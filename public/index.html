About Me

Hi! I am an incoming Computer Science PhD student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I will be working under the supervision of Prof. Josep Torrellas starting in Fall 2025. My research interests lie in computer architecture, hardware security, and domain-specific hardware accelerators.

Previously, I was an M.Sc. in Computer Science student at the University of Chile, advised by Prof. Alejandro Hevia and Prof. Luciano Radrigan. There I worked on cryptographic protocols for distributed systems and ML-enabled IoT nodes.

Outside of my studies, I enjoy hands-on projects, particularly tinkering with electronics, as well as hobbyist game development. I also enjoy cooking, hitting the gym, and reading manga.

Feel free to contact me through any of the provided links, and check out my blog where I write about what I'm up to!
